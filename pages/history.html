About Us

History

A chronological look at the founding and growth of St. Mary's College of Meycauayan.

Our Journey Since 1916

Historical information is based on official SMCM sources. Dates and events reflect the institution's documented history.

1916

Foundation

St. Mary’s College of Meycauayan was founded by a parish priest and was originally called Escuela Parroquial de Meycauayan. It initially offered a primary program with Catechism as its core subject.

—

Religious of the Virgin Mary

The school later came under the management of the Religious of the Virgin Mary (RVM), the first Filipino congregation founded in 1684 by Mother Ignacia del Espiritu Santo.

1929

Renamed

The school was renamed Escuela Catolica de Meycauayan.

—

World War II

Classes were suspended during World War II and later resumed.

1945

St. Mary's Academy

Classes, suspended since June 1944 due to the war, resumed in July 1945 as St. Mary’s Academy, offering Intermediate and First Year High School programs.

1949

First Graduates & Fire

The first ten high school seniors graduated in March 1949. The school building was also destroyed by fire on April 4, 1949, after which classes continued in private houses while reconstruction took place.

1981

New Four-Storey Building

A concrete four-storey building was completed in October 1981, marking the full transfer of the school — from kindergarten to high school — into one site. It added classrooms alongside administrative offices, a guidance office, science and reading laboratories, a library, a clinic, and audio-visual and conference rooms.

1986

Tertiary Education Begins

The institution opened its doors to tertiary education and was renamed St. Mary’s College of Meycauayan.

1990

First College Graduates

The college produced its first graduates.

1998

Our Lady of Lourdes Building

A new five-storey building for the College Department, named the Our Lady of Lourdes Building, was constructed to support the growing college population and the opening of the Hotel and Restaurant Management / Tourism programs. That same year, on November 30, 1998, the Grade School Department was granted PAASCU Level I accreditation.

1999–2001

Grade School PAASCU Level II

Following a Consultation Visit in October 1999 and a Preliminary Survey passed on January 17, 2000, the Grade School Department underwent Level II PAASCU accreditation on January 22–23, 2001, earning a five-year accreditation status.

2002–2003

ITE Program & ISO Certification

In June 2002, the Commission on Higher Education (CHED) granted a Government Permit for the college to operate its Information Technology Education (ITE) program. SMCM also began its ISO certification campaign in September 2002, completed its final audit in February 2003, and was awarded the ISO 9001:2000 Certification by TUV Rheinland on June 27, 2003.

2004–2005

High School & College PAASCU Accreditation

The High School Department was granted initial PAASCU accreditation on May 14, 2004. Government Recognition for the ITE program followed on February 15, 2005, and the College Department earned its own initial PAASCU accreditation on May 6, 2005.

2014–2015

IBEd Integration

The Grade School and High School departments were merged into the Integrated Basic Education Department (IBEd), unifying leadership under one Principal, Academic Coordinators, and Subject Area Coordinators.

2015–2016

Re-accreditation & Centennial

The newly-formed IBEd was granted PAASCU Level II re-accreditation on December 14, 2015. The following year, SMCM celebrated its 100th founding anniversary, opening the SMCM Museum as one of the milestone facilities prepared for the centennial.

2017 onward

Continued Growth

SMCM continued developing its Science and Technology programs, facilities, laboratories, libraries, and other learning spaces.
